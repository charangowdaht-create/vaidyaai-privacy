Privacy Policy — MediSamajh

Last updated: September 5, 2026

MediSamajh ("we," "our," or "the app") is developed by Charan Studios. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. We've written this to reflect exactly what the app actually does — not generic boilerplate.

1. Information We Collect

Account information (if you sign in with Google):
Your name, email address, and profile photo, as provided by Google. Signing in is optional — you can use the app's core features without an account by choosing "Skip for now."

Health-related content you provide:

  • Photos you take or upload of medicine labels, strips, packaging, and medical reports (blood tests, X-rays, prescriptions, and similar documents)
  • AI-generated summaries and explanations of the above
  • Medicine names, doses, schedules, and refill tracking information you enter
  • Reminders you set (medicine doses, checkups, custom reminders, water intake)
  • Conversations with the in-app health assistant ("Mitra"), including any report images or summaries you choose to attach to a question
  • Profiles for family members you choose to add, and any information you enter for them

App preferences:
Your selected language, notification settings, and similar in-app choices.

2. How Your Information Is Used

AI-powered features: When you scan a medicine or report, or ask the in-app assistant a question, the relevant image and/or text is sent to Anthropic's Claude API for real-time analysis, in order to generate the explanation or answer you see. We don't control Anthropic's own data handling for these API requests beyond what's described in Anthropic's own terms — you can review those directly at anthropic.com.

Cloud backup (if signed in with Google): Text-based data — your medicines, reminders, and report summaries — is backed up to Google Firebase (Firestore), tied to your Google account, so it can be restored if you reinstall the app or switch devices. Photos are not backed up to the cloud — they remain stored only on your device.

Local storage: By default, most app data (including all photos) is stored locally on your device, not on our servers.

3. What We Don't Do

  • We don't sell your personal or health information to anyone
  • We don't use advertising trackers or third-party ad networks
  • We don't collect your location
  • We don't store your scanned photos in the cloud

4. Third Parties We Work With

  • Anthropic — processes images and text you submit for AI-generated explanations and chat responses
  • Google / Firebase — provides sign-in and cloud backup of text data
  • Google Play Services — standard platform services for any Android app

5. Your Choices

  • You can use the app without signing in at all
  • You can sign out at any time from the Profile screen
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us (below)
  • You can decline any specific permission (camera, notifications) — some features may not work without it

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures, including encrypted transmission (HTTPS) for data sent to our servers and third-party processors. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

7. Medical Disclaimer

MediSamajh provides general information to help you understand medicines and medical reports. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified doctor or pharmacist regarding your health and medications.

8. Children's Privacy

MediSamajh is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under that age.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
