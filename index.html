Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 20, 2026

VaidyaAI ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the VaidyaAI mobile application.

In short: VaidyaAI scans medicines and medical reports using AI to help you understand them in your own language. Your scans are stored only on your device. We do not sell your data to anyone.

1. Information We Collect

VaidyaAI collects the following information to provide its core features:

  • Photos you capture or upload — images of medicines, prescriptions, and medical reports that you choose to scan.
  • Scan results — AI-generated summaries, translations, and explanations of your scanned medicines or reports.
  • Profile information — name, age, blood group, height, and weight, if you choose to enter them.
  • Language preference — the regional language you select for translations.
  • Family member profiles — names and health details you choose to add for family tracking, if you use this feature.

We do not collect your location, contacts, browsing history, or any data unrelated to the app's medicine and health-scanning features.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To analyze and translate scanned medicines and medical reports using AI.
  • To organize and display your scan history within the app (Reports tab).
  • To personalize the app experience, such as showing your name and preferred language.
  • To improve translation quality when you voluntarily submit feedback on a translation.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

Your scanned photos, scan results, and profile information are stored locally on your device using secure on-device storage. We do not maintain a central server database of your personal health scans.

When you scan a medicine or report, the image is sent securely to our AI processing partner (Anthropic's Claude API) solely to generate the explanation and translation. This data is processed for the single purpose of generating your result and is not used to train AI models or shared with third parties for advertising.

4. Third-Party Services

VaidyaAI uses the following third-party service to provide its core functionality:

  • Anthropic Claude API — used to analyze scanned images and generate plain-language explanations and translations. Image data sent to this service is used only to generate your scan result.

We do not use third-party advertising networks, analytics trackers, or data brokers.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or health data to any third party. Your scan history and profile data remain on your device unless you choose to share a scan result yourself (for example, by sending feedback via email).

6. Your Choices and Control

  • You can delete any saved scan at any time from the Reports tab.
  • You can edit or remove your profile information at any time from the Profile tab.
  • Uninstalling the app permanently removes all locally stored data, including scan history and profile details.

7. Medical Disclaimer

VaidyaAI provides general informational explanations of medicines and reports using AI. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations, and should never replace consultation with a licensed doctor or healthcare provider. Always follow your doctor's advice.

8. Children's Privacy

VaidyaAI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
